01 / Who We Are

WE'RE NOT TRYING TO LOOK LIKE A BIG PRODUCTION HOUSE.

We're trying to make something that feels real. Flixosol is a young group of creators exploring filmmaking, cinematography, editing, acting, design and visual storytelling. We learn by making, experiment with what we have and care more about the story than the size of the setup.

02 / What We Believe

AGE DOESN'T DECIDE QUALITY.

A good story doesn't become better because the person behind the camera is older. It becomes better because someone cared enough to find the right frame, the right performance, the right sound and the right cut.

That's the standard we're chasing. Not perfection on the first attempt — progress with every project.

03 / Our Principles
01

STORY OVER GEAR.

A camera is a tool. The idea behind the camera is what makes people stop, watch and remember.

02

EXPERIMENT WITHOUT FEAR.

We would rather try something strange than make the same thing everyone else is already making.

03

CREATE WITH INTENT.

Every frame, transition, sound and colour should have a reason to exist.

04 / How It Started
01 / THE IDEA

START WITH A STORY.

Flixosol began with the belief that young creators could make cinematic work without waiting for the perfect equipment, perfect team or perfect opportunity.

02 / THE EXPERIMENT

USE WHAT WE HAVE.

Phones, borrowed cameras, available locations, natural light and whatever else helps us get the shot.

03 / THE TEAM

FIND PEOPLE WHO CARE.

Cinematographers, editors, actors, designers and creators — different skills, one shared obsession with storytelling.

04 / THE NEXT FRAME

KEEP BUILDING.

Every project becomes another lesson, another experiment and another step toward making the kind of films we want to see.

05 / Our Manifesto

WE DON'T WANT TO WAIT FOR PERMISSION TO CREATE.

We want to learn, shoot, fail, improve, collaborate and tell stories while we're still figuring everything out.
